## Our lawful bases

- **Contract** — to provide the legal services you instructed us for (Article 6(1)(b) UK GDPR).
- **Legal obligation** — e.g. anti-money-laundering and record-keeping duties (Article 6(1)(c)).
- **Legitimate interests** — keeping the portal secure and keeping an audit trail (Article 6(1)(f)).
- **Special category data** (for example health information such as a TB test certificate, or religion shown on
  a marriage certificate) — necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims, including
  obtaining legal advice (Article 9(2)(f) UK GDPR).

- **Partner/sponsor accounts** — where a partner or sponsor creates their own account (see section below), the lawful basis for processing their data is **legitimate interests** (Article 6(1)(f)): supporting the main applicant's case.

## Partner and sponsor accounts

For some applications, UHA Legal may invite a third party — for example an employer, a financial sponsor or a family member ("partner") — to upload documents directly.

When this happens:

- The partner receives an invitation email and creates a separate account.
- At first sign-in the partner is shown a short privacy notice and must accept it before continuing.
- The partner can upload only the specific documents assigned to them.
- Their uploaded documents are visible to UHA Legal staff. Your caseworker may also make them visible to you once satisfied with the content.
- If the partner account is cancelled or your case is closed, the partner is signed out immediately and can no longer access the portal.

The partner's personal data (email address, uploaded documents, sign-in records, audit entries) is held for the same period as the rest of your case file and is subject to the same rights described below. If you upload documents that also name a third party — for example payslips from an employer — please share this notice with that person.

## How long we keep it

We keep your case documents and records for 6 years after your case closes, as required by our regulator, then delete them securely. Backups are deleted within a further 30 days.

## AI-assisted document review

To help our caseworkers work accurately and quickly, your uploaded documents may be read by an AI service.

The AI is used only by our caseworkers, never to give you advice directly. Here is what it does:

- It reads the text in your documents and flags things like expiry dates, names and document type.
- It checks whether the documents you have sent meet the immigration rules for your visa route.
- It can answer a caseworker's question about your case using text it finds in your documents.

A qualified caseworker reviews every AI finding and makes the final decision. No decision about your case is made by the AI alone.

The AI service we use is **[AWS Bedrock in Amazon's London data centre / Anthropic — confirm which]**. The provider does not use your data to train its AI models. Document text is sent only for the purpose of the analysis above and is not stored by the provider after the response is returned.

You can ask us to have your case reviewed without any AI tools. Email info@uhalegal.com.
